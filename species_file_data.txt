! This is a list of species in the system, followed by their binding
! energies, followed by their desorption energies, diffusion energies
! and bulk-diffusion energies.
!
! Following Garrod & Herbst 2006 top-layer diffusion is given by 
! Eb = 0.5Ed, with Ed being the sublimation energy. The last parameter,
! Eb2, is the bulk diffusion value and is 0.7Ed following Chang and Herbst
! 2012 and Chang and Herbst 2014. The values are in Kelvin. 
!
! NOTE: The electron is not given any diffusion/sublimation energies
! since it is not needed in the simulation.
!
! NOTE: The "*" character stands for an excited species. 
!
! Values of Ed for O2 come from Acharyya et al. 2007, the rest
! come from Allen and Robinson 1977 for neutrals. Charged
! species are given the same values as an approximation.
!
! **********************************************************
! The format is:
! #, NAME, Ed(K), Eb(K), Eb2(K)
!
! =>
!
! #, NAME, Ed(K), 0.5Ed(K), 0.7Ed(K)
!
! **********************************************************
